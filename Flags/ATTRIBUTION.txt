FactionGate flag assets
=======================

National flags (*.png matching ISO countries)
  Source: https://flagcdn.com/ (CDN of public-domain / freely redistributable national flags)
  Downloaded at build/setup time via tools/fetch_flags.ps1

BDF / PALA
  Custom Boscali Defense Force and Primeva Alliance flags provided by DarkSkies
  for FactionGate (join/map/hangar UI). Not extracted from game assets.

NATO
  User-provided NATO-style flag PNG (replaces earlier local placeholder).

USSR
  User-provided Soviet-style flag PNG (replaces earlier local placeholder).

UNSC / Insurrectionists / Super Earth / Automatons / Brotherhood of Steel / UKUS / AUKUS
  User-provided flag PNGs (replace earlier local placeholders).

Fiction factions (Chernarus, Takistan, CSAT, Ace Combat, Helldivers,
Marathon, C&C, Fallout, Warhammer 40k, Cyberpunk, etc.)
  Original solid-color label placeholders generated locally for join-UI use only.
  These are NOT official game assets. Drop replacement PNGs into this folder
  (same filenames) and press F10 in-game (or restart) to reload.

To add a new faction: add an entry to presets.json and a matching PNG here.
